Marquee Player · #01

Gaganjeet Singh Bhullar

Born 27 April 1988 · Amritsar, Punjab · 6 ft 1 in · Resides in Kapurthala

India's most decorated active golfer joins the Lions as our marquee — a 25-time professional winner and one of the most successful Indians in Asian Tour history. Bhullar turned pro in 2006, peaked at world No. 85 in March 2013, and was honoured with the Arjuna Award, India's second-highest sporting honour, the same year.

His eleven Asian Tour titles make him a fixture of that circuit's modern era. His lone DP World Tour win came at the 2018 Fiji International, and he made his single major-championship appearance at The Open at Turnberry in 2009. He was India's number-one amateur in 2004 and 2006, contributed to India's team silver at the 2006 Asian Games, and represented India at the 2024 Paris Olympics.

Introduced to golf at age four by his father, H.S. Bhullar, his career is a long, patient, ledger-style story — exactly the kind the Lions are built around.

Rising Star · #02

Harshjeet Singh Sethie

Delhi · Standing 6 ft 7 in · Product of the Delhi Golf Club

A genuinely unusual presence on the Indian tour — at six feet seven inches, Sethie cuts a silhouette the gallery learns to find on the practice tee before he ever reaches the first box. The height is a hook; the resume backs it up.

At 21, he claimed his maiden professional title at the Rs 1 crore Coimbatore Open 2023, beating the experienced Om Prakash Chouhan in a playoff. The win lifted him from 81st to 11th on the PGTI rankings in a single week and announced a young player who could close in big moments.

Sethie is the kind of signing a long-horizon franchise should be making — early-career, high-ceiling, with a story Chennai's fans can grow up watching.

Tour Pro · #03

Samarth Dwivedi

A confirmed touring professional with an Official World Golf Ranking profile and an active presence on the PGTI. The signature line on his current resume is a fourth-place finish at 7-under in an IGPL event during the 2025 season — proof that on the right week his number can lead a leaderboard.

Dwivedi rounds out the Lions roster with the kind of repeatable, tournament-tested professional who keeps a team in the conversation across a full season rather than a single hot week.
